        A match is a reason to verify. No match is not a green light.

        Each rule describes one scam pattern that an official source has warned about, for example a shipment message that asks you to update your details through a link. The desk compares your message with those rules in English and Arabic, and looks at any link for warning signs such as a hidden destination or a copy of a courier's name.

        If a rule matches, you get the reason, the source it comes from, and the steps to take. If nothing matches, you are told so. That never means a message is safe.

        What it can't do

        • It covers delivery and banking messages only. Prize, job, visa and government scams are not covered yet.
        • It can miss new scams, and it can flag a harmless message that happens to use similar words.
        • It is not an official fraud report. If you've lost money or shared a code, contact your bank and the police straight away.

        Your privacy

        • Your message is checked in memory. It is not saved, logged or sent back from the server.
        • Links are never opened. They are shown with the dots replaced, so they can't be tapped by mistake.
        • There are no accounts and no tracking.

        Rules and sources

        Every rule cites the official page it is based on, with the dates it was published and last checked.
